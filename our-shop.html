Our 5-Step Refill & Remanufacturing Methodology

01 — Circuit Check

Testing electrical resistance across copper contacts and inspecting internal sponges.

02 — Ultrasonic Flush

Removing dried ink plugs and spent waste toner powder in sealed chambers.

03 — Vacuum Fill

Precision air-free ink injection or polymer toner filling to full XL volume.

04 — Drum & Chip

Replacing worn green OPC drums, doctor blades, and firmware-current chips.

05 — Proof & Seal

Printing live CMYK swatch bars and sealing nozzles cleanly for ready-to-use pickup.
