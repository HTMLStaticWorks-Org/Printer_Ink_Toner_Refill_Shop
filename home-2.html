Our 5-Stage Cartridge Quality Assurance Workflow

01

Shell & Circuit Inspection

Checking cartridge seals, sponge condition, and electronic contact pins before refilling.

02

Ultrasonic Deep Flush

Clearing dried ink residue, waste toner buildup, and clogged micro-nozzles cleanly.

03

Metered OEM-Spec Fill

Injecting brand-calibrated pigment ink or high-density toner to full XL page capacity.

04

Smart Chip & Drum Reset

Installing firmware-ready chips and fresh OPC drums so ink-level monitors read accurately.

05

Live Test Print Certification

Running full CMYK color bars and crisp black text proofs before sealing for pickup.
