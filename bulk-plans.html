COMMON QUESTIONS

Bulk Plan & Refill Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about cartridge chips, printer warranties, turnaround time, and bulk office accounts.

Will refilled or remanufactured cartridges void my printer warranty?
No. Under consumer protection regulations (including the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act), printer manufacturers cannot void your hardware warranty for using refilled or remanufactured cartridges—and Inkcraft covers every cartridge with a 100% print guarantee.
How long does a walk-in inkjet or laser toner refill take?
Standard inkjet cartridges are vacuum-cleaned, refilled, and test-printed in 15 to 20 minutes while you wait. Laser toner rebuilds with OPC drum and smart-chip replacement are typically ready within 1 to 2 hours or available as an instant counter swap.
Will my printer recognize ink levels on refilled cartridges?
Yes, for supported HP, Canon, Brother, and Xerox cartridges we install updated or reset smart chips so your printer detects the cartridge cleanly and tracks ink or toner capacity accurately.
How many times can the same cartridge shell be refilled?
Most inkjet cartridges can be refilled 3 to 5 times as long as the internal sponge and copper circuit remain intact. Laser toner cartridges can be remanufactured repeatedly whenever we swap worn OPC drums and seals.
Do you offer free pickup and delivery for business bulk plans?
Yes. All Office Fleet Pro and Enterprise SLA bulk plan accounts receive scheduled weekly courier pickups for empty cartridges and doorstep delivery of fresh sealed refills at no extra delivery charge.
