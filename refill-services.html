Vacuum-Chamber Inkjet Cartridge Refilling

We evacuate trapped air pockets inside sponge and tank-based cartridges before metering OEM-matched dye or pigment ink. Each unit is pressure-tested, ultrasonically cleaned at the nozzle plate, and sealed for leak-free installation.

Laser Toner Refilling & OPC Drum Rebuilding

High-speed monochrome and color laser cartridges require more than toner powder. We clear waste hoppers, inspect magnetic developers, swap fatigued green OPC drums and doctor blades, and fit fresh chips for clean, streak-free pages.

Printhead Recovery & Nozzle Calibration

Streaks, faded colors, or missing black lines often come from dried ink inside thermal nozzles. Our heated ultrasonic recovery station dissolves blockages safely and verifies nozzle alignment across test patterns.

Same-Day Business Exchange & Recycling

For offices that cannot afford printer downtime, we pre-stock remanufactured cartridges matching your exact fleet models. Drop off empties for instant swap credits or schedule weekly office courier replenishment.
